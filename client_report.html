Reflection

Challenging Parts

The most challenging part was, of course, getting the CSS to match the wireframes. Getting the HTML down is relatively easy but then translating over the CSS and making sure it fit what the client wanted was difficult.

Enjoyable Parts

It's always nice to see the site come together and become a whole project. Like I said, the CSS was difficult but that is what brings everything together. Adding in the colors and fonts makes it all worth it.

Things I Would Do Differently

Something I would do differently is probably how I communitcated with the developers of my client site. I didn't make too many changes so I was pretty hands off so I think I would want to try and work more closely with them.

Working On Real World Projects

After working in theses teams, I definitely feel a lot more prepared to work on real world projects. Being communicative and flexible is important for working in teams and these projects definitely helped improved those skills.
